## On the Sideline

Everyone running MTL Hockey is a volunteer — coaches included, and many of them are barely more than another parent who was handed a bag of gear and a schedule. Two requests that keep game day enjoyable for the kids:

- **Give it 24 hours.** If you disagree with a coaching decision, wait a day before raising it with the coach or team manager, and do it away from the kids.
- **Please don't approach a referee about a call.** If you believe a game was officiated in a way the league should hear about, email [mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com](mailto:mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com) or use the [league contact form](https://mountaintopleague.com/contact-us/).

The full [24-hour rule](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/sportsmanship/), and the rest of what we ask of coaches and parents, lives on the [MTL Volunteering site](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/).

## Game Types & What You'll Need

MTL Hockey isn't one format — where and how your child plays depends on the division and the season, and so does the gear. Per-division equipment lists are still coming from the coordinators; what we can say today is below.

### Summer Street Hockey at O'Connor Park

The summer sessions at the [O'Connor Park rink]({{ site.baseurl }}/rinks/oconnor/) are essentially pick-up games. Kids who already know the game, or who are comfortable being dropped into a rink with little formal instruction, tend to thrive. Shy or entirely new players who need more instruction up front may find it a lot at first — if that sounds like your child, email [mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com](mailto:mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com) and we'll help find the right fit.

- What we've seen in past summers, not a requirement: most kids end up playing in **protective glasses and a mouthguard**, and full helmets, let alone gloves, tend to go unused in this setup. Whether anything is required is the coordinators' call — see below
- It's summer on an outdoor rink: **bring extra water**

> **Coordinator input needed:** which divisions or ages the summer sessions are open to, and whether any protective gear is required rather than customary.

### Full Ice

MCYHL league games at [Mennen Arena]({{ site.baseurl }}/rinks/mennen/), the Montclair State Middle School League, and sessions at [Codey Arena]({{ site.baseurl }}/rinks/codey/) are full-ice hockey.

> **Coordinator input needed:** the required equipment list for full-ice play, per division, and where families can borrow or buy gear.

### In-House Skills at the MTL League House

Squirts In-House Skills sessions run at the [MTL League House]({{ site.baseurl }}/rinks/mtl-house/).

> **Coordinator input needed:** what the sessions look like and what gear players need to bring.
