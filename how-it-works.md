## The Season

- Mites play a 20-game MCYHL league schedule at Mennen Arena, with MTL practices in between.
- Squirts run In-House Skills coaching at MTL House, with a Mennen league team formed if there is enough interest.
- Middle School plays a 10-game Montclair State Middle School League schedule; the season begins in early winter.

*Season dates and practice schedules are confirmed by the hockey coordinators before each season — this page will be updated as they land.*

## Registration

Sign-ups are rolling and all in one place on the [Register]({{ site.baseurl }}/register/) page. Early registration genuinely matters: ice time is purchased based on interest.

## What You'll Need

Skates, helmet with full cage, and protective gear; street hockey needs are lighter. *Detailed per-division equipment lists are coming from the coordinators.*
