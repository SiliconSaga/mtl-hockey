- Middle School plays a 10-game Montclair State Middle School League schedule; the season begins in early winter.

*Season dates and practice schedules are confirmed by the hockey coordinators before each season — this page will be updated as they land.*

## On the Sideline

Everyone running MTL Hockey is a volunteer — coaches included, and many of them are barely more than another parent who was handed a bag of gear and a schedule. Two requests that keep game day enjoyable for the kids:

- **Give it 24 hours.** If you disagree with a coaching decision, wait a day before raising it with the coach or team manager, and do it away from the kids.
- **Please don't approach a referee about a call.** If you believe a game was officiated in a way the league should hear about, email [mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com](mailto:mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com) or use the [league contact form](https://mountaintopleague.com/contact-us/).

The full [24-hour rule](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/sportsmanship/), and the rest of what we ask of coaches and parents, lives on the [MTL Volunteering site](https://volunteering.mountaintopleague.com/).
