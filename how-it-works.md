## Game Types & What You'll Need

MTL Hockey isn't one format — where and how your child plays depends on the division and the season, and so does the gear. Per-division equipment lists are still coming from the coordinators; what we can say today is below.

### Summer Street Hockey at O'Connor Park

The summer sessions at the [O'Connor Park rink]({{ site.baseurl }}/rinks/oconnor/) are essentially pick-up games. Kids who already know the game, or who are comfortable being dropped into a rink with little formal instruction, tend to thrive. Shy or entirely new players who need more instruction up front may find it a lot at first — if that sounds like your child, email [mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com](mailto:mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com) and we'll help find the right fit.

- In practice most kids end up playing in **protective glasses and a mouthguard** — full helmets, let alone gloves, tend to go unused in this setup
- It's summer on an outdoor rink: **bring extra water**
