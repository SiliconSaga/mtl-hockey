**MCYHL league games** for Mites (and the Squirts Mennen-league option) are played at the William G. Mennen Sports Arena in Morristown.

[Open in Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Mennen+Sports+Arena+Morristown+NJ)

161 East Hanover Ave, Morristown, NJ — about 25 minutes from West Orange.
