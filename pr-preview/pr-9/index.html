MTL Hockey

MTL Hockey is part of the Mountain Top League, an all-volunteer organization serving the children of West Orange, NJ since 1959. We offer ice hockey and street hockey programs — hometown rec hockey, all fun.

Where We Play

Street hockey at O’Connor Park, full ice at Codey Arena, league games at Mennen Arena in nearby Morristown — we’re a rec league, not a travel league, though not every rink is in West Orange. Maps and details on the Rinks page.

Get Involved

MTL is run entirely by volunteers. To coach, manage a team, or help out, email mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com.
